Dog Duo

Two characters who are linked by something deeper than fate, with a very distinctive leashed but animalistic flavor. There is dog, collar or leash imagery somewhere in the pairing; general animal imagery is an acceptable stretch. When the bond stops being figurative it usually becomes Soul Manipulation or Hive Mind and Unification.
