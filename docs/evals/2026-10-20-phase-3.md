# Phase 3 measurements, 2026-10-20

Model `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017` through OpenRouter, reasoning at the
model default, temperature 0.3, concurrency 8. Library: 776 works and 10
series. All costs are what OpenRouter reported.

## Dossiers

| Run | Subjects | Calls | Time | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full build | 786 | 1189 | 51 min | $1.17 | 719 strong, 58 partial, 9 none; 994 Tumblr notes attached; 4 malformed replies, all recovered on retry |

The nine the model did not know are 2026 releases and small albums. Text
sources (23 works) produce `text`-depth dossiers with chapter locators.

Structured output: sending `response_format: json_schema` made the model
degenerate (one Pantheon dossier: 75 arcs, 132k output tokens, 8 minutes,
truncated). Prompt-only JSON validated locally took 9 seconds and filled
every list. `TROPES_JSON_SCHEMA` is therefore off.

Reasoning: `TROPES_REASONING=off` is about eight times faster per call (1 s
against 9 s on a small pass-1 batch) but scored a clear Dog Duo case lower
(0.2 against 0.55). Left at the model default; the knob exists for the eval.

## Eval (`tropes-worker eval`), v3 prompt

| Trope | Verified truth | Seed agreement | Mean true score | Proposed ≥ 0.5 beyond truth | Precision at 0.5 | Time | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sapient Ship or Station | 5 (all seeds) | 1.0 | 0.95 | 36 | 0.12 | 36 s | $0.33 |
| Dog Duo | 5 (all seeds) | 1.0 | 0.93 | 17 | 0.23 | 42 s | $0.31 |
| Casual Necromancy | 3 (all seeds) | 1.0 | 0.92 | 8 | 0.27 | 44 s | $0.23 |

Under v1 the mean true scores were 0.74, 0.69 and (Sapient Ship) 0.89; under
v2 they fell to 0.53 and 0.18 for Dog Duo and Casual Necromancy, with two of
five Dog Duo truths under the cutoff. v3 pins the owner's examples above 0.9
while keeping the proposal list a fraction of v1's.

Caveat that matters: every verified instance today is one of the owner's seed
examples, and v3 shows the seed entries to the model, so "seed agreement 1.0"
is expected rather than earned. The eval reports non-seed recall separately
and it will only mean something once model-found instances have been
verified or removed. The "noise" column, proposals above the cutoff that are
not verified instances, is the number to watch across prompt and model
changes; it is not precision, because some of those are right.

## What to tune next

- `TROPES_PASS2_MIN_SCORE` 0.3 and `TROPES_PASS2_CAP` 60: every scan fills the
  cap, so the floor is not binding. Raising the floor to 0.5 would cut cost and
  noise once pass-1 scores are trusted; check against the eval first.
- Broad tropes (Sapient Ship) will always propose many; the review page's
  "unchecked below" line is the lever there, per trope if needed.
- The three nostalgebraist novels lack text; Almost Nowhere scored 0 to 0.3 in
  pass 1 for a trope it seeds. The EPUBs from AO3 would fix that.
